  • Support workshops, reviews and career preparation.

Donate

Invest in skills, mentorship and cultural preservation.

Donations support digital learning materials, mentorship coordination, community workshops, heritage documentation and the future Innovation & Technology Centre.

  • KES 1,000 can support learner resources.
  • KES 5,000 can help run a training session.
  • Institutional gifts can sponsor cohorts or equipment.

Donation details are submitted securely for follow-up.
