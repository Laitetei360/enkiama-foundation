Programs

Practical pathways for digital confidence.

Five programs connect young people to skills, mentorship, innovation spaces and cultural pride.

Pathways

Designed for the moment between ambition and opportunity.

Programs combine hands-on learning, career support and community grounding so youth can move forward with skill and identity.

Flagship

Enkiama Digital Academy

Training in computer basics, coding, AI, data science, web development, graphic design, digital marketing and freelancing.

  • Best for beginners and early-career youth
  • Focuses on portfolio-ready practical skills
Apply Now

Career & Mentorship Hub

Mentorship, career guidance, CV support, interview preparation, internships and job readiness.

  • Connects youth with working professionals
  • Builds confidence for school, work and enterprise
Become a Mentor

Heritage Preservation Initiative

Documenting Maasai language, oral stories, traditional knowledge, art, music and community history.

  • Supports cultural memory and pride
  • Uses digital tools for archiving and learning
Explore Heritage

Innovation & Technology Centre

A future physical hub in Kajiado County for learning, research, innovation and community problem solving.

  • Creates a local center for digital learning
  • Supports research and community solutions
Support Centre

Women & Girls in Technology

Supporting girls and young women to access digital education, mentorship and leadership opportunities.

  • Reduces barriers to technology access
  • Builds confidence, leadership and peer support
