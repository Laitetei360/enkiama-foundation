About Enkiama

A foundation built on hope, identity and skill.

Enkiama means Hope in Maa. We empower Maasai youth through technology, mentorship, innovation and cultural preservation.

Who we are

Community-rooted. Future-facing. Youth-led.

Enkiama Foundation was founded by Caleb Laitetei, a Software Engineer and Data Scientist from Enkariak-Rongena, Loitoktok, Kajiado County.

The foundation exists to bridge Maasai heritage and modern technology so young people can grow into digital opportunity while carrying their identity with pride.

Mission

Empower youth with tools for the digital age.

To empower Maasai youth through digital skills, mentorship, innovation, career development and cultural preservation.

Vision

A future rooted in culture and ready for the world.

A future where Maasai youth are digitally skilled, economically empowered, culturally rooted and globally competitive.

Our values

The principles behind every program.

The work is practical, but the heart is human: opportunity should strengthen identity, not erase it.

Hope

We create opportunities for young people to believe in and build a better future.

Heritage

We protect language, stories, knowledge and cultural pride for future generations.

Innovation

We use technology to solve community problems and open new career pathways.

Mentorship

We connect youth with guidance, encouragement and real-world readiness.

Excellence

We set high standards for learning, leadership, stewardship and trust.

Community

We work with families, elders, mentors, schools and partners to create durable change.

When youth can code, tell their stories and honor where they come from, the future becomes something they can shape.

Why it matters

Digital access should reach rural talent too.

Many young people have the ambition to learn and lead, but lack access to devices, mentors, training spaces and career networks. Enkiama is designed to close that gap with dignity and cultural confidence.
