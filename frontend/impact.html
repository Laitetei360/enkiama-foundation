Who we serve

Starting with Maasai youth aged 18-30.

Our initial focus is recent secondary school graduates, university students, graduates and young professionals across Maasai communities.

As Enkiama grows, the foundation will also support other underserved communities that need digital access, mentorship and culturally respectful opportunity.

Impact model

What changes when youth gain access.

Digital ability

Youth gain practical skills in computers, coding, data, design and digital communication.

Career direction

Mentorship helps young people understand options, prepare applications and pursue work.

Cultural confidence

Heritage work strengthens pride, language continuity and community memory.

Impact is not only counted in numbers. It is seen when a young person has the courage, skill and identity to choose a better path.
