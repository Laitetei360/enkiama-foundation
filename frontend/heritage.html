Heritage

Preserving Maasai memory with digital care.

We protect Maa language, oral stories, traditional arts and community wisdom using respectful digital tools.

Digital archive

Culture carried forward by community voices.

Heritage preservation is not nostalgia. It is a way to keep language, wisdom and identity alive for young people building modern futures.

Maa Language

Building digital resources for Maa words, meanings, pronunciation and learning materials.

Oral Stories

Recording elders' wisdom, community stories, songs, proverbs and historical memories.

Traditional Arts

Showcasing beadwork, attire, symbols, dances, music and Maasai creative heritage.

Respect first

Preservation must honor the people who carry the knowledge.

Stories submitted to Enkiama should be shared with permission, context and respect for family, elders and community ownership.

Technology becomes meaningful when it helps a community remember, teach and imagine together.

Community archive

Submit a story, word, memory or lesson.

Share a heritage story, community reflection, learning experience or technology journey. Your submission helps grow a respectful archive of knowledge and hope.

  • Use your own words or submit with permission.
  • Include enough detail for future learners to understand the context.
  • We may review submissions before publishing or archiving.
